Canvas Offscreen Resampling

Bitmaps are drawn into memory-backed HTML5 Canvas elements where target dimensional transformations are calculated. High-performance browser rasterizers encode output byte arrays directly into Blob constructs before issuing direct browser download streams.

FAQ

Are my photos uploaded when I compress them? No. Compression runs on Canvas in this tab. Download the result from your device.

Isolation Note
