GDPR & KVKK Compliance Statement / GDPR ve KVKK

Effective Date: October 7, 2026

1. Scope and local file processing

Mustafa Göksal operates OpenStackTools as an independent software developer and individual project operator. Selected files and entered content are processed in volatile browser memory on the user's device. The application is designed not to upload file contents to an OpenStackTools file-processing server. Browser extensions, the user's device, hosting requests, and third-party services may involve separate processing.

2. Veri sorumlusunun kimliği / Controller

Veri Sorumlusu / Data Controller: Mustafa Göksal, Independent Software Developer & Project Operator (individual / natural person). Notice region: Nilüfer / Bursa, Türkiye. A full street address is withheld for personal privacy; formal written notices are accepted by privacy email or supplied upon request. Contact: gogsalmustafa19@gmail.com.

3. Purposes, categories, and legal bases

Hosting providers may process connection and security data such as IP address, requested URL, timestamp, and browser/network headers to deliver and protect the site. The operator uses browser local storage for language and consent preferences. If the user explicitly opts in, Google AdSense may process identifiers, IP address, device/browser data, cookie data, ad requests, and interactions. Depending on the applicable jurisdiction, optional advertising is based on consent; legal bases for other processing depend on the actual hosting and deployment. Frontend resources are also requested from third-party CDNs (including Tailwind CSS and pdf-lib for the PDF tool), which may receive connection metadata.

4. VERBİS registration status

The controller declares that they are exempt (Muaf) from the VERBİS registration obligation as a natural-person operator, pursuant to applicable Personal Data Protection Board decisions concerning natural persons and SMEs below the statutory employee-count and annual financial-balance thresholds. This declaration applies only while the controller and processing activities continue to satisfy the applicable exemption criteria; it must be reassessed if those facts or rules change.

5. Yurtdışına Veri Aktarımı / International transfers

Ads are disabled until explicit user opt-in. Following opt-in, Google AdSense and relevant Google entities or partners may receive technical and advertising data and process it in countries outside Türkiye, potentially including the United States and other locations where Google operates. Specific recipients, destinations, transfer mechanisms, and safeguards depend on the current account configuration and Google contractual terms. The applicable transfer mechanism must be verified for the live deployment before enabling ads. Hosting and CDN providers may separately process connection information under their service arrangements and in their operating locations. File contents are not intended to be sent to the operator's file-processing servers.

6. Saklama, haklar ve başvuru / Retention and rights

Language and consent preferences remain in browser storage until cleared. Hosting and advertising providers determine their own retention periods. Under KVKK Article 11 and, where applicable, GDPR Articles 15–22, individuals may exercise applicable access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, and complaint rights. Submit requests to gogsalmustafa19@gmail.com. Formal notices are accepted by email or upon request; a full street address is withheld for personal privacy.
