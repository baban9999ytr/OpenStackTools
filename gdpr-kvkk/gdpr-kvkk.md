## Processing, purpose, and categories

Selected files and entered text are processed locally in volatile browser memory to provide the chosen tool. The application is designed not to transmit file contents to an OpenStackTools file-processing server.

Static hosting providers may process connection and security data (for example, IP address, requested URL, timestamp, and browser/network headers) to deliver and secure the website. Frontend assets are also requested from third-party CDNs, including Tailwind CSS and pdf-lib for the PDF tool, which may receive connection metadata. The browser stores language and consent preferences locally. After a visitor explicitly opts in to advertising, Google AdSense may process IP address, browser/device information, identifiers, cookie data, ad requests, and interactions for advertising delivery, measurement, and related purposes. Exact categories and purposes depend on the live configuration and must be kept current.

Depending on the purpose, jurisdiction, and actual provider arrangement, applicable legal bases may include consent for optional advertising and a lawful basis for technically necessary site delivery and security. The controller must confirm and disclose the basis applicable to each deployed processing activity; this statement does not replace that assessment.

## Recipients and international transfers

Hosting providers receive network requests to serve the site. Advertising data is not sent to Google through this application unless the visitor opts in and the deployed runtime configuration enables AdSense. Following opt-in, Google entities and relevant partners may process information in countries outside Türkiye and the EEA, potentially including the United States and other countries where the service operates.

The actual recipient entities, destinations, transfer mechanism, and safeguards depend on the current AdSense account, Google contractual terms, and deployment. The operator must verify the transfer mechanism under current Turkish law and, where applicable, GDPR before enabling ads, and should identify the applicable mechanism and destination in the live notice. Hosting and CDN providers may separately process connection data in their service locations.

## Retention

Language and consent choices are stored in the visitor's browser until cleared. The consent record stores the choice, purpose, policy version, and timestamp. The application does not transmit that record to the operator. Hosting and advertising providers set their own retention periods under their current notices and contracts.
