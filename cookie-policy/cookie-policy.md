The site uses browser `localStorage` for interface language and consent preferences. The advertising consent record includes the visitor's choice, purpose, policy version, and timestamp. This browser storage remains on the device unless cleared and is not sent to the operator by the application.

## Optional Google AdSense

The AdSense runtime script and ad `<ins>` elements are created only after an explicit advertising opt-in. If a visitor rejects or has not made a choice, the runtime is not loaded and no ad elements are created. After opt-in, Google and advertising partners may read or set cookies or similar technologies and process IP address, device/browser information, identifiers, ad requests, and interactions.

Google may process data outside Türkiye/EEA, potentially including the United States. The specific Google entities, destinations, retention periods, and legal transfer safeguards depend on the production account configuration and current contractual terms. The operator must verify these details and the applicable consent requirements before enabling advertising.

## Change or withdraw consent

Visitors can change or withdraw their advertising choice through **Cookie settings** on the site. Browser storage can also be cleared through browser settings. Google ad personalization can be managed through [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads), subject to Google's available controls.
