Cookie & Advertising Policy

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

1. Overview

OpenStackTools operates as a pure client-side utility platform. We do not use first-party tracking cookies to log user identity or behavior. This policy explains how local storage and third-party advertising cookies function on our site.

2. Essential Local Storage

We utilize the browser's standard localStorage API solely to store functional interface preferences (such as language settings: EN/TR). This data remains strictly inside your web browser and is never transmitted to any external server.

3. Third-Party Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense)

To keep OpenStackTools free and open-source without user fees or subscription paywalls, we display non-intrusive advertisements provided by Google AdSense.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

4. Opting Out and Managing Cookies

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users can opt out of third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.
