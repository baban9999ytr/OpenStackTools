System Architecture

The Problem

Most online utility platforms operate by uploading your private files to remote servers. This introduces artificial processing queues, bandwidth constraints, and severe privacy risks for sensitive documents.

The Solution

OpenStackTools shifts the compute entirely to the client. By leveraging modern browser APIs (WebAssembly, WebCrypto, HTML5 Canvas), all processing occurs synchronously within your device's RAM.

Common questions

Do you upload PDFs or images?

No. Tools read files into this page’s memory, process them with browser APIs, and let you download the result. OpenStackTools does not operate a file-processing server.

Is there an upload size limit?

We do not set a cloud quota. Large files are limited by your device RAM and the browser tab. That is why a “free online PDF merger no upload limit” search matches this architecture.

How do ads work?

Google AdSense loads only after you accept cookies. You can change that anytime with Cookie settings. File tools work whether ads are on or off.

Compliance & Governance

Data Minimization

We do not operate backend processing servers. Files loaded into OpenStackTools are processed locally and discarded by your browser's garbage collector upon tab closure. No personal data is collected or transmitted.

Monetization & Cookies

To maintain the platform independently, we utilize Google AdSense. AdSense may use cookies to serve personalized advertisements. No user-identifiable tracking is implemented within the application logic itself.
