How does local processing work?

The tools use browser capabilities such as Canvas, Web Audio, WebCrypto and pdf-lib. The page still makes normal web requests to load the site and its dependencies. Those requests do not include the files you select for processing.

Hosting providers may receive ordinary connection metadata, such as an IP address, when serving the site. This is separate from the contents of files you process.

Common questions

Do you upload PDFs or images?

No. Tools read files into this page’s memory, process them with browser APIs, and let you download the result. OpenStackTools does not operate a file-processing server.

Is there an upload size limit?

We do not set a cloud quota. Large files are limited by your device RAM and the browser tab. That is why a “free online PDF merger no upload limit” search matches this architecture.

How do ads work?

Google AdSense loads only after you accept cookies. You can change that anytime with Cookie settings. File tools work whether ads are on or off.

Compliance & Governance

Data Minimization

OpenStackTools does not receive the contents of files you select in the browser tools. The hosting provider and services needed to deliver the website may process normal connection metadata. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Monetization & Cookies

Google AdSense is loaded only after you opt in through the consent controls. Ad and consent services may use cookies and process data under their own policies.
